Soldiers and gear

A soldier is whoever holds a post in a watchtower or a barracks. Only soldiers fight in a raid or an attack; an ordinary resident never does. A soldier lives where they serve, and there are no ranks: what makes a soldier strong is their gear alone.

Gear

A soldier wears gear in three places — weapon, shield and armour — one piece in each. The carriers bring it from the store as they bring a tool.

  • A sword replaces a spear. Once a sword lies in the store, a soldier with a spear takes it and gives the spear back, whole, into the store.
  • Gear wears out by the day, not by the fight. Every piece a soldier wears loses one day at every change of day, and at 0 it is used up.
  • When a soldier falls, what they wore is lost. A soldier who leaves their post any other way puts their gear back into the store.
  • The settlement keeps one of each piece ready for every soldier's post, and one more. So your toolmaker makes a spear and a shield even before you have a tower. A post whose soldier has a sword gets no spear made for it.
  • Gear is not handed in unless the server allows every good; the settlement makes its own — see Handing goods in.

In a duel

A soldier without gear has the health and the blow in the table on Raids; each piece of gear changes one of them. A weapon makes them hit more often and harder, armour gives them more health, and a shield makes the raider miss more often, though never below a floor. A raider has neither shield nor armour.

Gear decides the fight. A guard without gear almost never beats a raider, one with spear and shield mostly does, and one with all three usually beats three in a row.

What you see

  • A soldier carries what they have: the weapon in one hand, the shield in the other, the armour on the body.
  • At night a soldier walks a round of their building with a torch; by day they are at their post. No other post has a night round.
  • The buildings tab names every soldier of a watchtower or barracks with their health against the full and the days their gear has left.
