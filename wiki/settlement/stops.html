Messages

When a building stops

A marker floats over the building for as long as the cause lasts. Every message is one of three kinds:

  • A blockade stands until you act.
  • A waiting message stands as well, but the settlement is already clearing it itself — a delivery on the road, enough in stock, a tool being made.
  • A hint means the building works, only worse than it could.

By default only blockades go into the chat, so a line in the chat means somebody has to do something. Each player chooses that for themselves with the button under the list What it is waiting for on the overview: every click moves on to the next of Chat: blockades only, Chat: blockades and waiting and Chat: off. Hints never go into the chat. The choice is kept on your own game, across a restart too. Only players with a role in the settlement see any of it. M hides the markers over buildings and road sites, and pressing it again brings them back; the chat is not affected — see Controls. What the messages mean:

A hint is not a standstill. The building goes on working, only worse than it could, and the marker says why. When you join a game, every blockade that is still standing is said once in the chat, so you do not walk into a stopped settlement without a word — and, with Chat: blockades and waiting, every waiting message too. With Chat: off nothing is said.

Running short

The settlement warns you before its food or its fuel runs out. At the start of every day it compares what it holds — in the store, in every building and on the road — with the stock three days before. Food is bread, fish and fruit counted together.

  • The warning starts when the stock has fallen and, at that pace, lasts less than three more days.
  • It ends when the stock stops falling, or lasts at least six days again.
  • Once the stock is empty, the houses speak instead: “has no food left; its people will move away if none arrives”.
  • Nothing is warned in the first three days of a settlement.

When the warning starts, one line goes into the chat to everybody with a role in the settlement — “Oakhill: Food is running short. The stock has fallen over the last 3 days and lasts about 2 more days.” It goes with the blockades, so Chat: off silences it. There is no marker over a building for it; the overview shows the stock of food and fuel every day, in gold while the warning stands — see The charter screen.
