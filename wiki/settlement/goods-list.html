Reading the table

  • Weight is how many slots one unit takes in the store, and how much of a carrier's load it fills. The charter holds 2 400 slots, every storehouse 1 200 more; a carrier carries 12 a trip, 36 with a cart.
  • Hand in as is what the goods port takes: one item is one unit. Only the basic goods go in; the others go in only if the server allows every good, and the column says so — see Handing goods in. An item that is no good at all is refused with “is not something this settlement stores”.
  • Made until the store holds is the ceiling above which nobody makes the good any more — see Only so much is made. A user is a building that takes the good in, a post that works with it as a tool, or, for building material, a builder's post. The ceiling bounds only what your people make; what you hand in yourself is never refused while there is room.
  • A village pays is how much of the good a village takes for its emeralds, in whole lots — see Trade Post. A good with a dash is not taken, and raiders never carry it off.
  • Bread, fish and fruit are one kind to a house. Each counts as food, and a house takes whichever there is — see Food.
