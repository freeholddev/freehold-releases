Items and blocks

Items

ItemWhat it is
CharterA rolled deed between two wooden rods, sealed with an emerald. Hold it to place a settlement — see Founding a settlement. Crafted from an emerald framed in eight planks of any wood, see below; in creative mode it is in the Freehold tab
Hammer, Saw, Chisel, Bellows, Brew KettleThe tools vanilla has no item for. Each has a crafting recipe, and your settlement makes them too. Hammer and saw are basic goods and go in at a goods port; chisel, bellows and brew kettle only if the server allows every good — see Handing goods in
CartA carrier's hand cart. It has a crafting recipe, and your toolmaker builds it too; it goes in at a goods port only if the server allows every good — see Goods and carriers
Flour, BeerGoods of the settlement that vanilla has no item for. They have no crafting recipe, and the goods port takes them only if the server allows every good

Every other good, the emerald and a soldier's gear included, stands for a vanilla item — which ones, and which of them go in at a goods port, is on All goods.

The charter recipe

An emerald in the middle of the crafting table, eight planks around it — any wood, mixed if you like — make one charter. The recipe shows in the recipe book as soon as you carry an emerald, and it is the only Freehold recipe there. A datapack can replace it like any vanilla recipe.

Blocks

None of these can be crafted, and none can be broken while it belongs to a building or a road.

BlockWhere you find it
Goods PortThe barrel-like block in an outside wall of the charter and of every storehouse. Right-click with something in hand to hand it in — see Controls
Building AnchorThe block of carved stone inside every building, one each. Right-click opens the charter screen on that building, sneak and right-click tears it down
March StoneOn every chunk corner of your boundary — see March stones
Road LanternBeside every crossing of two roads: a wooden post with a torch at an earth crossing, a gallows with a hanging lantern at a wooden or stone one — see Once the route is finished
Spent Torch, Spent Wall TorchA building's torch while the building is not working: charred, no flame, no light — see The fire in a building
Spent LanternA lantern of a level 2 building while the building is not working: sooted glass, no light, hanging or standing like the lantern it stands for. It has no item and drops nothing — see The fire in a building
Chimney SmokeInvisible, above a chimney: smoke rises from it while the fire below burns
Windmill SailsThe sails of a windmill. They always turn
Oak Leaves with ApplesThe leaves of a bearing orchard tree. It behaves like oak leaves and has no item
Sack of FlourScenery in the mill. It holds nothing; the flour is in the store
Stripped log slabsHalf-height beams in the woods of the biomes, used in the drawings of the buildings
Dev markersFive blocks for blueprint authors — entrance, home, road link, storage access, work. They do nothing in a settlement and show only to operators

The creative tab

Everything Freehold adds as an item is in its own creative tab, "Freehold", behind the spawn eggs, with the charter as its icon, so the creative search finds all of it. In order: the charter; the saw, hammer, chisel, bellows and brew kettle; flour, beer and the cart; the seven stripped log slabs and the sack of flour. The five dev markers are only there for operators who have the operator items tab switched on, the way vanilla shows its command blocks.

Survival is not affected: there the charter is crafted.
