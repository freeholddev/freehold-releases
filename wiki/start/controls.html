Controls

Every gesture and key Freehold adds. Everything else works as in vanilla Minecraft.

GestureWhereWhat happens
Right-click, something in handgoods portHands that stack into the store, as much of it as fits — basic goods only, see Handing goods in
Right-click, empty handgoods portSays what the block is for, and where the store is read
Hopper or pipegoods port, any sideThe same as handing in by hand; what is not taken stays in the hopper. Nothing comes out of the store into a hopper
Press VanywhereOpens the charter screen of the settlement you are standing in; in one you hold no role in, its card
Press ManywhereHides the messages over buildings and road sites, or shows them again; a line over the hotbar says which. The choice is remembered. The chat and the raid marker are not affected — each has a button of its own on the overview
Press Zwhile placing a mine or an adit mineSwitches the seam the ore map shows: coal, iron, coal again. It is the key left of X, which a German keyboard labels Y. Rebindable under “Freehold” in the controls menu — see Mine
Hold NanywhereShows, for every settlement in sight, its border as a line on the ground and its name, both in the settlement's own colour; and over every building its name and number — “Sawmill 1”, “House 3” — in the same colour and a little under its message if it has one, sites included. Let go and all of it is gone — see Seeing the borders
Right-click, empty handanchor blockOpens the same screen on that building. In a settlement you hold no role in it opens its card instead, and under a watchtower there stand the lines to attack it — see Attacks
Sneak + right-clickanchor blockAsks whether to tear that building down
Sneak + right-clicka site whose anchor block is not built yet, any block of itThe same question for that site
Right-click, empty handany block of a roadSays the road's grade and its trips over the last days, and what it still lacks to rise — see Reading a road
Sneak + right-clickany block of a roadAsks whether to tear up that stretch of road
Sneak and lookat a roadShows in red the stretch that would go
Hold the charteranywhereStarts placing a settlement
Pick a buildingcharter screen, build tabStarts placing that building, or drawing a road
Right-clickwhile placingPlaces the building where the model stands
Scroll wheelwhile placingTurns the building — all but the village gate, which faces the settlement by itself
Sneak + scroll wheelwhile placingLooks through the versions of the building
Walk, look aroundwhile placingMoves the building; it stays ahead of you
Move the mouse up or downwhile placing or drawing a roadTilts the view, from flat to steep. The building stays where it is
Sneak + right-clickwhile placing an ordered buildingCancels the order
Put the item awaywhile placing the charterCancels
Right-clickwhile drawing a roadSets a point of the route
Sneak + right-clickwhile drawing a road, on a building or a roadFinishes the route
Escapewhile drawing a roadCancels the route
Click ^ or vcharter screen, build tabMoves that site one place up or down the queue
Click the filter buttoncharter screen, buildings tabOpens a list of building kinds; a click on a kind shows only that kind, a click elsewhere or Escape closes the list without a change
Click - or +charter screen, buildings tabTurns a stock target down or up, for every building of that kind on the same level
Shift + clickthe same buttonsTen at a time instead of one
Click *charter screen, buildings tabPuts that one target back to the default of that level
Click - or +charter screen, politics tabMoves a policy, a share of a scarce good or the ore share of the mines five per cent down or up
Shift + clickthe same buttonsTwenty-five per cent at a time instead of five
Click *charter screen, politics tabResets that policy to its default: 100, and 50 for Upgrade before new building; by a ranking, resets the order or the shares to their starting values; by the ore share, to 50 to 50
Click ↑ or ↓charter screen, politics tabMoves a kind of building one place up or down the list of where a free resident goes

The three keys can be moved in the controls menu, in the category Freehold: Open the charter screen, Toggle messages over buildings and Show building names and borders (hold).

The goods port is the barrel-like block in an outside wall. The charter has one, and so does a storehouse once you build one — but the store behind them is one and the same, so it makes no difference which of them you hand a stack to.

The anchor block is the block of carved stone inside a building. Every building has exactly one. Right-clicking it opens the charter screen on the buildings tab with that building already open (in a settlement you hold no role in, the card of that building): the anchor picks, it does not open a menu. Sneaking tears that one building down.
