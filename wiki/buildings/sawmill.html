Sawmill

Costs32 planks
Building work400 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 6 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolSaw, one per worker
Makes2 logs → 3 planks every 60 s
Keeps in stock12 logs — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds6 planks — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Sawing trestle
Level 22 workers; keeps 18 logs in stock
Upgrade to 216 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 400 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The sawmill saws logs into planks, the material nearly everything is built from.

  • Two lumberjack huts keep one sawmill busy. A hut fells a tree of four logs every four minutes, and the sawmill saws two logs a minute — so the second hut is what doubles your wood, and a second sawmill only pays once a third and fourth hut stand.
  • It shares its logs with the charcoal pile. When there are not enough to go round, each gets its share of the loads, set on the politics tab — see Who gets a scarce good.
  • It stops when the settlement has enough planks, and starts again the moment a new site takes some. Planks are what fills a store, because hardly anything but building spends them — see Only so much is made.

Level 2

At level 2 the sawmill has a second post: a second sawyer works at a bench of their own, with a saw of their own, so the sawmill saws twice as much and wants twice the logs. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
