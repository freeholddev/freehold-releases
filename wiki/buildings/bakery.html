Bakery

Costs20 planks, 16 ashlars
Building work400 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 6 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolShovel, one per worker
Makes3 flour + 1 fuel → 3 loaves every 6 min
Keeps in stock6 flour, 3 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds4 loaves — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Bakehouse
Level 22 workers; keeps 9 flour, 5 fuel in stock
Upgrade to 210 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 400 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The bakery fires its oven once and bakes a batch: three sacks of flour and one unit of fuel make three loaves, in six minutes, with a shovel from your toolmaker. It is the end of the grain chain:

  • It burns fuel. An oven has to be lit, so bread hangs on your charcoal pile or your mine as well as on the farm and the mill. One firing to three loaves, so the drain is small — but a settlement with no fuel at all bakes nothing. The fishing hut and the orchard are still the ways to food that need none of it.
  • It bakes in threes. Flour and bread move in batches of three, so an oven that is one sack short stands still with two in the bin. One farm and one mill keep one bakery busy.
  • It costs ashlar — 20 planks and 16 blocks of it — so a stonemason, and behind it a quarry, have to be standing before you can build one.
  • Your people eat it. Bread, fish and fruit are all food — see Food — and a house without any of them empties out. The bakery stops by itself once the settlement has enough lying ready.

Level 2

At level 2 the bakery has a second post: the two places a baker goes between on level 1 become two posts, one baker at each. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
