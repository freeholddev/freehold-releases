Farm

Costs24 planks, 12 ashlars
Building work400 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 6 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolHoe, one per worker
Makes2 wheat every 60 s
Makesa patch of 2 × 2 sown every 40 s
Fetched once it holds6 wheat — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Reach14 blocks
Grows15 min after planting
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsBarn, Granary
Level 2works at 150 % pace
Upgrade to 212 planks, 12 ashlars, 2 fittings; 400 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 48 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The farm tills the land around itself. It needs no seed from you and no water.

  • Its fields are plots of four by six, laid where there is room within 14 blocks. The farm looks for whole rectangles of grass or dirt with daylight over them, nearest first, and keeps clear of buildings and roads. A pond or an outcrop costs a whole plot. A farm on stone, sand or under trees has nowhere to sow and says so ("has no free planting spot left") — so in a desert, place it on grass or dirt by a river or at the edge of the biome.
  • Two farms close together split the land between them. Each column belongs to the nearer farm, so a farm built close to another has less land of its own.
  • A wheat field of your own within a farm's reach belongs to the farm. Ripe wheat there is reaped once, unripe wheat is pulled up for nothing, and any farmland outside the farm's plots turns back to dirt. Other crops, such as carrots, are left alone.
  • The nearest plot is tilled whole before the next is opened, so the tilled land grows with the hands the farm has and not with its radius. That is why a young farm has one field and an old one has several.
  • A reaped field stays a field. Bare farmland on a farm's plots does not dry out, so the plots stay visible between two crops.
  • The farm grows its own wheat, whether anyone is watching or not. A field is ripe 15 minutes after it was sown. The wheat on a farm's fields does not grow the vanilla way, so standing next to it changes nothing. Bone meal from your own hand still ripens a field.
  • Reaping and sowing take turns, a patch of two by two at a time: two wheat for a reaped patch, and the freed ground is sown on the next round. The seed comes back with the harvest, so you never deliver any.
  • Waiting for the wheat is not a standstill. While every field is sown and nothing is ripe yet, the farm shows no message and keeps its farmer.
  • The windmill is what eats the wheat. Until you have one, the farm stops by itself once 32 are lying ready, and it does not sow for a harvest it would not bring in.
  • Tear the farm down and the fields stay — farmland, crops and all. Without the farm they are ordinary vanilla fields again.
  • The farmer and the forester share the hoe, so a farm means one more hoe from your toolmaker.

Level 2

At level 2 the farm works at 150 % pace: sowing a patch takes 27 seconds instead of 40, and harvesting two thirds of its time. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches. Over the hay hatch of the barn a beam juts out with a chain — the hay hoist.
