Brewery

Costs24 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings
Building work440 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 7 min 20 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolBrew Kettle, one per worker
Makes3 wheat + 1 fuel → 6 beer every 6 min
Keeps in stock6 wheat, 3 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds6 beer — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, With Kiln

The brewery malts its own grain and brews it: three wheat and one unit of fuel make six beer, in six minutes, with a brew kettle — the one tool your smithy forges. It is where your wheat goes besides the mill.

  • It costs 24 planks, 16 ashlar and 4 fittings. The fittings are what your smithy makes, so the iron chain comes before your first brewery. Only the brewery and the tavern cost them.
  • Wheat has two buyers, the mill and the brewery, the way raw wood goes to the sawmill and the charcoal pile; when it is short, each gets its share — see Who gets a scarce good. One brewery takes about ten wheat a day; with a bakery running as well, two farms are busy.
  • The kettle comes from your smithy, ahead of fittings nobody needs. A kettle lasts forty brews, about twelve days. You can craft one yourself as well (see Tools), so a settlement without a smithy is not stuck.
  • Beer is not food. Nobody gets full on it and a house does not take it as a meal.
