Storehouse

Costs48 planks
Building work500 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 8 min 20 s for one builder with a hammer
Store1 200 slots
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Hoist dormer
Level 2a store of 1 800 slots
Upgrade to 224 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 500 work units in 2 stages; once 12 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem, the whole store at least 75 % full

A storehouse is a second door, not a second pile. Build one and the settlement gets 1 200 more slots — but the goods stay in one heap that every building shares, so nothing is ever "in the wrong store". What a second storehouse really buys is distance: a trip that runs over a store runs over whichever one is nearer to the building at the other end, so a storehouse out by the forest halves the walk to the huts around it and leaves everything else as it was. Put it where the carriers are walking furthest.

It has a goods port of its own, and what you hand in there goes into the same store as at the charter. It is also the answer to a full store: the ceiling on production bounds what your people make, never what you hand in yourself, so a great reserve wants another storehouse. And a building more than 256 blocks from every store and from everything it trades with, and with no road, cannot be reached at all; a storehouse within range, or a road to a building that is reached, is the way out — see Goods and carriers.

Level 2

At level 2 the storehouse holds 1 800 slots instead of 1 200. It rises only while the whole store of your settlement is at least three quarters full — after one storehouse has risen there is room again, and the next waits until the room runs short. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
