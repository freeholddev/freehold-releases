Lumberjack

Costs24 planks
Building work300 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 2 builders at once — about 5 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolAxe, one per worker
Makes4 logs every 4 min
Fetched once it holds2 logs — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Reach32 blocks
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Saltbox roof
Level 2works at 150 % pace
Upgrade to 212 planks, 12 ashlars, 2 fittings; 300 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 48 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The lumberjack fells the trees within its reach and brings back raw wood — the start of every wood chain. The raw wood goes to the sawmill and the charcoal pile. New trees come from the forester, who needs nothing from the lumberjack.

  • An orchard's trees are not the lumberjack's. A lumberjack never counts or fells a tree on an orchard's spot — only once the orchard is torn down do they become wild trees like any other.
  • A felling gives four logs, and the lumberjack stops when the settlement has enough raw wood. "Enough" is the ceiling on All goods; it says has stopped: the settlement has enough and starts again once the wood is spent — see Only so much is made.
  • Two huts match a sawmill for the raw wood.
  • Felling is real and it is not a drop. The lumberjack takes the nearest tree within 32 blocks, trunk and branches; the leaves are left to decay on their own. Nothing falls on the ground — the wood goes straight onto the hut's pile, for a carrier to fetch. The clearing grows outwards from the hut, so you can see what you have used up.
  • The forest runs out. Two lumberjacks fell as fast as one forester plants, and a new tree takes ten minutes to grow. When the radius is empty, the lumberjack stops and says so — and the forester needs a fifth resident, which needs a house, which needs planks.
  • Every hut counts its own circle. Two lumberjacks far apart do not share a forest: one can stand idle with nothing left within its 32 blocks while the other fells all day, and the one that has run out is the one that says so. The same holds for the forester — it plants in its own circle, so a clearing it cannot reach stays a clearing. Where you put the huts decides which of them keeps working.
  • A settlement keeps running when you leave, but trees that grow or fall around the hut while nobody is near are only noticed once somebody comes back.

Level 2

At level 2 the lumberjack works at 150 % pace: every tree takes two thirds of the time. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
