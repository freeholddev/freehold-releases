Fishing Hut

Costs28 planks
Building work300 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 5 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolFishing Rod, one per worker
Makes1 fish every 3 min
Fetched once it holds4 fish — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Reach16 blocks
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site; 48 blocks from the next building of its trade; at least 24 columns of open water within 16 blocks; a step faster every 48 more, up to 4 times
VersionsStandard, Boathouse
Level 2works at 150 % pace
Upgrade to 214 planks, 12 ashlars, 2 fittings; 300 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 48 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The fishing hut catches fish, a food that costs nothing but planks and a rod. It has to stand at the water, and the reason is its jetty.

  • The last three rows of its ground plan are the jetty, and they have to be water. The placement preview shows the whole plan; turn it until those three rows lie over the lake, the river or the sea and the rest of it on the bank. If any of them is on land, the preview says "The jetty needs open water; part of it would lie on land." and nothing is placed.
  • The bank may rise up to three blocks above the water; it is cut down to the height of the hut when the ground is levelled. Shallow water behind the jetty, up to two blocks deep, is filled in.
  • The water under the jetty is left alone, and the fisher stands out at the end of the boardwalk rather than in the hut.
  • How much water is around decides how fast it fishes. Every column of open water within 16 blocks counts, once each — surface, not depth, so a deep ocean is worth no more than a broad lake. Under 24 columns it does not fish at all and says "has too little water within its work radius"; from there it gets a step faster every 48 columns, up to four times the speed. A pond of five by five blocks is the smallest thing that works; open sea is as good as it gets.
  • Nothing runs out. Fishing takes nothing out of the water and changes nothing in the world, so a hut at a good spot keeps its speed for ever. If you want more fish, build a second hut — but two fishing huts have to stand 48 blocks apart, so one small lake will not take four of them.
  • Your people eat the fish. It is food in a house the same as bread — see Food. It stops by itself once enough is lying ready — 32, and four more for every house.

Level 2

At level 2 the fishing hut works at 150 % pace, on top of what the water around it gives. A hut on plenty of water that catches a fish every 45 seconds on level 1 catches one every 30. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches. Lanterns stand on the posts of the boardwalk at Standard, on the post of the jib at Boathouse.
