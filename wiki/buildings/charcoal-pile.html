Charcoal Pile

Costs20 planks
Building work300 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 5 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolShovel, one per worker
Makes3 logs → 2 fuel every 3 min
Keeps in stock12 logs — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds4 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Twin clamp
Level 22 workers; keeps 18 logs in stock
Upgrade to 210 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 300 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The charcoal pile chars three raw wood into two units of fuel, in three minutes, with a shovel from your toolmaker — the same tool the baker carries. One pile keeps a settlement of about thirty residents in fuel, houses, bakery and brewery included. Besides a mine on coal it is the only way to fuel, and fuel is what torches are made of, so it is also the building that opens up new land:

  • It costs 20 planks and nothing else, so you can put one up before you own any fuel at all.
  • It eats raw wood, and so does your sawmill. When there is not enough to go round, it gets its share of the loads and the sawmill the rest — see Who gets a scarce good. If your planks dry up after you build one, give the sawmill more on the politics tab, or build another lumberjack.
  • Nothing is taken out of the world. Like the bakery it works only on what is delivered: wood in, charcoal out, no radius, no digging.
  • It stops when there is enough fuel lying around. With a toolmaker burning fuel into torches it keeps going; with nobody wanting any, it fills the store to its ceiling and then idles, saying it "has stopped: the settlement has enough".
  • Its clamp really burns. The mound in the yard has a fire in its core and a vent at the top. It is not a fire hazard: it will not spread and it does not go out in rain. It does go out when the clamp stops — no wood, no collier, or a full pile — and lights again when it starts.

Level 2

At level 2 the charcoal pile has a second post: a second collier works beside the first, with a shovel of their own. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
