Builders' Yard

Costs40 planks
Building work500 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 8 min 20 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts2 builders
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Low build
Level 23 builders
Upgrade to 220 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 500 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

Two more builder's posts. Your charter has one; every builders' yard adds two.

  • Its hammers come from your own toolmaker. The charter's kit covers only the first builder, so a builders' yard stands half idle — slowed, not stopped — until your toolmaker has made two more hammers. See Tools.
  • More builders fill the first site, then start the next. A site only holds so many — two at a small building, three at a middling one, four at a large; each building's page says how many. Builders always go to the first site in the queue that can be worked on, so a builders' yard finishes the site in front sooner, and once you have more builders than that site holds, the rest walk on to the next one and your settlement raises two at a time.
  • A builder between two sites waits at the yard while material is on its way, and goes home when there is no site or its material is missing altogether.
  • A builder walks to their site before the work there begins, over finished roads where that is quicker — see Walking on it.
  • How builders divide between buildings, roads and the workshops is set on the politics tab of the charter screen.
  • Every builder's post raises what the settlement keeps ready of the material it builds with — planks and ashlar — so the sawmill and the stonemason work longer before they stop.

Level 2

At level 2 the builders' yard has three builder's posts instead of two. It rises like a workshop: an empty builder's post or a builder without a hammer holds it back, and the buildings tab gives the same reasons as at a workshop. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page. The third builder needs a hammer of their own.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: the panels of its walls are masoned, and lanterns instead of torches hang at the door and inside.
