The smithy forges iron ingots into fittings — bands, hinges and nails —, into the brewer's kettle and into a soldier's sword and armour, at the forge behind its open front. It is the last building of the iron chain.

  • Fittings are what the later buildings cost. The brewery and the tavern are built with them, and your toolmaker needs two for every cart. Without a smithy there are no fittings, unless the server allows every good and you hand in iron nuggets yourself.
  • It forges whatever the settlement is shortest of, and a building site waiting for fittings or a brewery without a kettle comes ahead of anything nobody needs.
  • A sword and armour make a soldier much stronger. Once a sword lies in the store, a soldier gives back their spear for it — see Soldiers and gear.
  • It burns fuel, like the smelter before it — one unit a job.
  • It costs ashlar, so your quarry and stonemason come first. Its smith uses a hammer, the same tool as the toolmaker and every builder.

Level 2

At level 2 the smithy has a second post: two smiths work at once, one at the forge and one at the anvil, where on level 1 one smith goes between the two; each needs a hammer of their own. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • An iron bracket juts out beside every opening, a lantern hanging from it — two at the open front or the gate, two at the door. Standard is stone on level 1 already; at Ridge louvre the timber frame is filled with masonry.
