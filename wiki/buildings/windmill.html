Windmill

Costs18 planks, 24 ashlars
Building work460 work units, the material paid in 3 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 7 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolShovel, one per worker
Makes3 wheat → 1 flour every 40 s
Keeps in stock12 wheat — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds4 flour — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site; nothing within 16 blocks more than 12 blocks over its ground
VersionsGallery mill, Smock mill
Level 22 workers; keeps 18 wheat in stock
Upgrade to 210 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 460 work units in 3 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The windmill grinds three wheat into one sack of flour, in forty seconds, with a shovel from your toolmaker — the same tool the baker and the collier carry. It stands between the farm and the bakery in the grain chain, which runs over three trades:

  • The bakery takes flour, not wheat. Without a mill the farm fills your store with grain that nothing can use. Farm, mill, bakery — in that order.
  • One mill keeps about three bakeries going. Grinding takes a third of the time baking does, so a mill is not a bottleneck unless you have many ovens; two or three farms keep one busy.
  • It needs an open stand, and that is the one rule no other building has. A mill is refused if anything within sixteen blocks of it reaches more than twelve blocks over the ground it would stand on — a hillside, a cliff, your charter, a watchtower, another mill. The refusal says how far over that line the tallest thing beside it stands, so walk until the number shrinks and then goes. An ordinary house is short enough to stand right next to one.
  • It is checked once, when you set it, and never again. Build a tower next to a standing mill and you have taken its wind; nothing will stop you and nothing will complain.
  • It costs ashlar and no ironwork — 18 planks and 24 blocks of it. Bread never needs iron.
  • The sails turn whether it is working or not. They are a picture of the wind, not of the work, and they turn on every mill in the world at a different angle.

Level 2

At level 2 the windmill has a second post: a second miller works on the milling floor, where the flour sacks stand against the east wall. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • It shows its level on the whole tower, because its base is masoned on level 1 already. The Gallery mill has a railing of iron bars round its gallery and lanterns on the wall above it; the Smock mill has a sack hoist on a chain over its sack hatch and lanterns on the four corners of its stone ledge.
