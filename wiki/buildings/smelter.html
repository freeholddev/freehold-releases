Smelter

Costs24 planks, 20 ashlars
Building work420 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 7 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolBellows, one per worker
Makes2 iron ores + 1 fuel → 1 iron ingot every 5 min
Keeps in stock12 iron ores, 6 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds4 iron ingots — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Hooded hearth
Level 22 workers; keeps 18 iron ores, 9 fuel in stock
Upgrade to 212 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 420 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The smelter is the end of the iron chain: two iron ore and one unit of fuel make one iron ingot, in five minutes, with a bellows from your toolmaker. It is the third building of the iron block and the only thing that has any use for ore.

  • It costs 24 planks and 20 ashlar. The furnace is masonry, so it is paid for in masonry — your quarry and stonemason come before your smelter. The two buildings that get you to iron, the charcoal pile and the mine, still cost planks alone.
  • Ore is lost to the slag. Two ore to the ingot, not one, so the mine is the bottleneck of the chain rather than the smelter.
  • Nothing is taken out of the world. Like the bakery and the charcoal pile it works only on what is delivered: ore and fuel in, iron out, no radius, no digging.
  • It shares fuel with every other fire in the settlement — hearths, toolmaker, bakery, brewery, smithy — and nothing else uses ore. When fuel is short, each workshop gets its share — see Who gets a scarce good.
  • The iron goes to your smithy, which forges fittings out of it and the brewer's kettle — nothing else uses it. Until a smithy stands it fills its shelf in the store, and at 32 ingots the smelter stops, saying the settlement has enough, and behind it the mine and the charcoal pile once their own shelves are full. Build the smithy and the chain starts again by itself.
  • Its fire really burns. The furnace in the yard has two mouths, one at the tapping floor where the smelter stands and one facing the road. It will not spread and it does not go out in rain — but it does go out when the smelter stops for want of ore, fuel or a worker, which is how you can tell a running bloomery from an idle one across the valley.

Level 2

At level 2 the smelter has a second post: a second smelter works at the ingot bench in the hall, while the first stays at the furnace outside, each with a bellows of their own. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • Its walls are masoned instead of boarded, and two lanterns hang at the gate.
