Barracks

Costs40 planks, 24 ashlars
Building work520 work units, the material paid in 3 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 8 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts4 soldiers
Beds4
Stands byits soldiers fight for every watchtower, charter and village gate within 96 blocks, anchor to anchor, and fill a fallen guard's post in a watchtower within that reach first
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Rampart

The barracks houses soldiers who stand by the watchtowers around it. It holds no land — that is the watchtower's — but its soldiers take a fallen guard's place and fight beside the towers' guards when raiders come. It costs planks and ashlar and no fittings, so a settlement can defend itself before it has an iron chain.

What its soldiers do

  • They are the reserve. When a post in a watchtower within reach falls free — a guard falls, or a tower has just been built — a soldier of the nearest barracks moves up to it first, with their gear and their wounds. The barracks' own post that comes free is then filled like any other.
  • They are the reinforcement. When a watchtower, the charter or a village gate within reach is attacked, the soldiers of the barracks fight after those of the target. The soldiers of every watchtower in reach do the same — see How a raid is fought. Against an attack by another settlement, the barracks in reach are all that defend the charter.
  • They go on attacks. A barracks gives all its soldiers to an attack on a foreign watchtower or charter within its reach — see Attacks.
  • They live where they serve. Each of the four posts has its bed in the barracks, and each soldier carries the gear the settlement brings them — see Soldiers and gear.
  • At night they walk a round of the barracks with a torch, as a watchtower's guard does.

What it looks like

The barracks is a stone hall behind a drill yard, with a palisade and a gate towards the road — that is how you tell it from afar. The soldiers drill in the yard. Standard has a gable roof, Rampart a roof you can walk on, with merlons all round and a ladder up from the yard.

The chests of Rampart are scenery. They have nothing to do with the store, and whatever you put into them is lost when the barracks is repaired or torn down.

On the charter screen

The buildings tab names every soldier with their health against the full and the days their gear has left, and the watchtowers within reach of the barracks.
