Charter

CostsNothing — it raises itself in about 60 s, without builders
Posts1 builder
Beds4
Store2 400 slots
Brings4 residents and a kit of 2 hammers, 1 axe, 1 saw, 2 hoes, 1 fishing rod
Carriersone for every 4 residents, at least one; 12 slots of weight a trip
Newcomersone a day while a fed bed is free; every bed of a supplied house adds 1 % to that pace, times the house's level
Villagesfound within 3 chunks of the boundary; every bed of a connected village adds 4 % to that pace, weighted by its reputation
Village tradea village trades 1 emerald a day for every bed it has, shared by every trade post
Reputation+1 for every emerald traded, −2 a day, at most 200; a village's beds count once at 0 and rise evenly to 200 % at 200

Level 2a store of 3 600 slots
Upgrade to 224 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 600 work units in 5 stages; once 12 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The heart of a freehold: its store, its first homes, and the place material is handed in. You place it yourself — how, and where it may stand, is on Founding a settlement.

  • It is the only building that raises itself, without material and without a builder, and the only one that brings people: four residents move in the moment it stands.
  • It cannot be torn down. It is the freehold itself; sneaking and right-clicking its anchor says so and does nothing.
  • Once no watchtower of the settlement stands that has had a guard, another settlement can attack the charter, and if it falls, the settlement ends — see Attacks. It is defended by the soldiers of every barracks in reach.
  • Its four beds need no food, and its people never leave — which is what keeps a settlement without food alive. See Food.
  • One of its beds belongs to its builder. The charter has one builder's post, and whoever takes it lives there; see Residents.
  • Its goods port is where you hand things in, and its store is the one every storehouse adds to. See Goods and carriers.
  • It sets the pace of newcomers. People move in at the rate in the table above while a bed with food is free, and a village you have connected adds to it — see Villages and how people move in.
  • It holds your first land. Watchtowers push the boundary out from there.
  • Without a watchtower that has had a guard it is the target of a raid, and with no soldier near it the raiders plunder the store at once — see Raids.
  • The charter item is crafted from an emerald framed in eight planks — see Items and blocks.

Level 2

At level 2 the charter holds 3 600 slots instead of 2 400. It is the one building that is never held back by a problem: empty beds or an empty store do not stop it, and it rises as soon as enough goes on around it and it has run its day. It pays and is built like any other upgrade — the rules are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page. Its land stays the same.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: the panels of its walls are masoned, lanterns hang instead of torches, and its tower is masoned all the way up.
