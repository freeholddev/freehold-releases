Production chains

Which building feeds which. The numbers are on each building's page and in All buildings; every good is on All goods.

Wood

Lumberjack → logs. The forester plants new trees so that the forest grows back; it needs nothing delivered. The logs go to the sawmill → planks, which nearly every building is built from, and to the charcoal pile → fuel.

Tools

Planks → toolmaker → every wooden tool, the bellows, a soldier's spear and shield, and torches (with fuel). With fittings from the smithy it also builds carts; you can also make one yourself. The brew kettle comes from the smithy. See Tools.

Fuel

Two sources: the charcoal pile chars logs, a mine or adit mine on coal hews it. Fuel is burnt by the toolmaker (torches), the bakery, the brewery, the smelter, the smithy and every occupied house (its hearth). Torches are what a watchtower is built from — so no fuel, no new land.

Stone

Quarry or opencast quarry → rubble → stonemason → ashlar, which the farm, the windmill, the bakery, the smelter, the smithy, the brewery, the tavern, the trade post and the barracks are built from. The village gate is built from the rubble itself.

Food

The short ways: fishing hut → fish, and orchard → fruit. Both cost only planks.

The long way: farm → wheat → windmill → flour → bakery (with fuel) → bread. It needs ashlar for three of its buildings, and fuel for the oven.

Both end in the houses, where your people eat. See Food.

Beer

Wheat → brewery (with fuel and a brew kettle) → beer → tavern, which makes the people of the houses around it work faster. Wheat then has two buyers, the mill and the brewery.

Iron

Mine or adit mine → iron ore → smelter (with fuel) → iron ingots → smithy (with fuel) → fittings, brew kettles, swords and armour. Fittings are what the brewery, the tavern and the cart cost.

Trade

Village gate and a road to it → a connected village → trade post, which sells the settlement's surplus to the village for emeralds and buys bread, fish, fuel, iron ore, iron ingots and wheat with them. So a settlement without a mine can still get iron, and one without a farm wheat.

Defence

Toolmaker → spear and shield, smithy → sword and armour → the soldiers of the watchtowers and the barracks, who fight off the raiders. See Soldiers and gear.

What has to stand first

  • Planks alone build: lumberjack, forester, sawmill, toolmaker, house, storehouse, builders' yard, quarry, opencast quarry, stonemason, fishing hut, orchard, charcoal pile, mine and adit mine.
  • Rubble as well: the village gate — so a quarry or an opencast quarry.
  • Torches as well: the watchtower — so a toolmaker and a source of fuel.
  • Ashlar as well: farm, windmill, bakery, smelter, smithy, trade post, barracks — so a quarry and a stonemason.
  • Fittings as well: brewery and tavern — so the whole iron chain, from the mine to the smithy.

The build tab shows the rule: a building stays grey until every good it costs can reach the settlement — in the store, as a basic good you may hand in, from a building of yours that makes it, or bought by a trade post. A placed building counts from the moment it is placed, so you can plan a chain ahead: place the stonemason, and the bakery can be chosen before the first ashlar exists.
