Adit Mine

Costs32 planks
Building work360 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 6 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolPickaxe, one per worker
Makes1 iron ore every 4 min
Makes1 fuel every 3 min
Fetched once it holds4 iron ores, 4 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site, and rise towards its working side up to 6 blocks above its floor; the ground 10 blocks behind its working side at least 3 blocks higher; at least 26 blocks of ore in its band it can reach under 2 blocks of rock
VersionsStandard, Timbered mouth
Level 2works at 150 % pace
Upgrade to 216 planks, 12 ashlars, 2 fittings; 360 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 48 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The mine for a hillside. Where it may stand is explained under Building against a hillside.

  • It drives into the mountain instead of sinking a shaft. It is the smallest building there is, and the building is the mouth: you walk straight through it and on into the mine. A drift runs level into the hill, and six blocks in stands a shaft with a ladder. From it the galleries branch out on levels five blocks apart, up into the rock as readily as down, timbered and lit like a mine's. Walk in and you will find the miner at the far end of them.
  • Its band stands twelve layers above the yard and eight below, twelve blocks around the shaft, and only the half of that which lies inside the hill.
  • Above the yard it stays inside the mountain: it leaves at least two blocks of rock between its galleries and the open air. Ore nearer the surface than that stays in the rock and is not counted, so a low hillside holds less to mine than it seems.
  • It needs 26 blocks of ore it can reach before it may be set, and the preview names them seam by seam as the mine's does. It works the same two seams in the same proportion, and a seam runs out the same way — see Mine.
  • “Worked out” means nothing is left within its reach. When it says “has no X left within its reach”, ore can still be seen in its galleries: outside the band, or too close to the surface. That ore stays where it is.
  • The ore map is rougher than the preview. The map counts all round each chunk's middle, the preview only the half inside the hill at the spot you aim at — so go by the preview. The map itself is described under Mine.
  • Its mining area never overlaps that of another mine, the ordinary kind included. Its area is the half circle of twelve blocks inside the hill, seen from above — see Mine. A mine behind its mouth is not in the way; one the drift runs towards needs more room. Where the areas would overlap, move the adit mine further off or turn it.

Level 2

At level 2 the adit mine works at 150 % pace: a block of iron ore takes 160 seconds instead of 240. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • It goes on working the same drift; the upgrade leaves the workings alone.
  • A worked-out deposit keeps it from rising, like any other standstill: “has no X left within its reach” counts as a problem.
  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches. The stone lintel over the portal juts out on both sides and carries the lanterns, and the same at the back; at Timbered mouth they hang on beam ends of pit timber. A lantern hangs under the open lean-to, over the sorting bench.
