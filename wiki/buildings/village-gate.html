Village Gate

Costs12 planks, 8 stones
Building work120 work units, the material paid in 1 stage; up to 2 builders at once — about 2 min for one builder with a hammer
Link roadan earth road from the gate in a straight line to the nearest village path, at most 32 blocks away
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard

The village gate connects a neighbouring village to your settlement. Only a connected village sends you people, trades with your trade posts and is protected like your own land — what that brings is on Villages. The gate has no post and nobody works in it.

Where it stands

A gate stands on your land, right beside the land of a village. A village's land is every chunk with one of its beds in it and one chunk all round; the gate's chunk has to touch it edge to edge. Three refusals belong to the gate alone:

ReasonWhat to do
A village gate stands on your land right beside the land of a village.Move it to the edge of a village's land; push your boundary there with a watchtower first if you have to
This village is already connected to a settlement, or one of your gates is already placed for it.A village takes one gate; see below
No village path lies within N blocks of the gate for it to link up with.Move the gate nearer to the village's paths

The gate turns itself. Its front always faces the settlement, away from the village, and the scroll wheel does not turn it.

The link road

The settlement lays a link road from the gate into the village by itself. When you place the gate it plans an earth road from the gate in a straight line to the nearest block of village path, at most as far as the table above says, over your land and the land of that village only. It is built once the gate stands, and runs out through the gate's frame into the village.

  • It is the one stretch of road outside your land. Every road you draw stays on your land; the link road is laid by the settlement and never drawn.
  • It is not part of your road network. It counts no trips, makes no crossing, does not rise to wood or stone, and cannot be read, torn up or upgraded with the road gestures.

Connecting the village

A village is connected while the gate stands, its link road is finished and one of your finished roads ends at the gate. So after the gate, draw an ordinary road from the settlement to its front. Until all three hold, the gate says what is missing:

  • “X is waiting for its link road into the village to be laid.” — a waiting message; the builders are on it.
  • “X does not connect its village yet: draw a road from the gate into the settlement.” — a blockade.
  • “X does not connect its village: another gate holds it already.” — a hint.

A village is connected to one settlement at most — whoever comes first. A second settlement can only connect it once the first loses the connection: its gate is torn down, its road to the gate is gone, or its land no longer touches the village.

The gate takes everything with it. Tear it down, and the link road and the road that ends at the gate go too, as they do with any building.

On the charter screen

The buildings tab says where the gate stands: “This gate stands at no village.”, “Will connect a village of N beds once it stands.”, “Connects a village of N beds; reputation there R.”, or what is still missing. It also names the soldiers that stand by the village, and after a lost raid how long the village still rests: “After a lost raid it brings neither people nor trade for 1:00 h more.” — see Raids.
