House

Costs32 planks
Building work600 work units, the material paid in 4 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 10 min for one builder with a hammer
Beds4
Keeps in stock8 food, 2 fuel — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Eats1 food per resident and day; a resident leaves after 2 days without
Hearthburns 1 fuel a day
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Porch
Level 26 beds; keeps 12 food, 2 fuel in stock
Upgrade to 216 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 600 work units in 4 stages; once 12 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

Room for four residents — six once it is upgraded — and a larder they eat out of. A house is what lets your settlement grow: newcomers only move in while a bed is free.

  • Only a house with food in it takes anybody. A carrier keeps it stocked with food — bread, fish or fruit — out of your store, and its people eat one a day each. A house whose larder stays empty loses its people one by one — see Food.
  • Its hearth burns fuel, one unit a day, delivered like the food — but not ahead of the workshops: the fuel for houses is shared out with the bakery, brewery, smelter, smithy and toolmaker, see Who gets a scarce good. On level 1 a cold hearth changes only the light and keeps the house from rising; the overview of the charter screen counts them. See The fire in a building.
  • A house has no post, but its people work near it. Nobody works in the house itself; a worker lives within 64 blocks of their post, while carriers and builders live wherever a bed is free — see Where people live.

Level 2

A house rises to level 2 once it has been full and supplied for a whole day in a busy neighbourhood — every bed taken, food and fuel in it, and 12 people working and living within 32 blocks around it. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the numbers are in the table at the top of this page.

  • Six beds, and a larder of twelve. Three beds stand downstairs and three upstairs; the house keeps 12 food instead of 8, and its fuel stays at 2. Both count from the moment the last stage is built — until then its people live on in the four beds of level 1.
  • It has an upper floor with three of the six beds, and each version furnishes it differently.
  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: the ground floor is masoned, and lanterns hang at the entrance and inside in place of torches.
