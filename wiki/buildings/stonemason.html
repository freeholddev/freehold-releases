Stonemason

Costs32 planks
Building work400 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 6 min 40 s for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolChisel, one per worker
Makes2 stones → 1 ashlar every 90 s
Keeps in stock8 stones — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Fetched once it holds4 ashlars — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Mason's lodge
Level 22 workers; keeps 12 stones in stock
Upgrade to 216 planks, 16 ashlars, 4 fittings; 400 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 32 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The stonemason squares two rubble into one block of ashlar, with a chisel from your toolmaker. Ashlar is what the masonry buildings cost — the farm, the windmill, the bakery, the smelter, the smithy, the brewery, the tavern, the trade post and the barracks; All buildings has how many blocks each. What is not built lies in the store, and like rubble the stonemason stops by itself once enough is lying ready — 32, and 16 more for every builder's place your settlement has.

It shares its rubble with your building sites. When there is not enough to go round, the sites together get their share of the loads and the stonemason the rest — see Who gets a scarce good.

Level 2

At level 2 the stonemason has a second post: a second mason works at a banker of their own against the east wall of the workshop. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
