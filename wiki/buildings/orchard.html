Orchard

Costs24 planks
Building work300 work units, the material paid in 2 stages; up to 3 builders at once — about 5 min for one builder with a hammer
Posts1 worker
ToolHoe, one per worker
Makes2 fruit every 60 s
Makesan oak planted on a free spot every 2 min
Fetched once it holds6 fruit — a default, changed on the buildings tab
Reach16 blocks
Grows10 min after planting
Bears20 min after it has grown, and again 20 min after every picking
Plantationgroups of 2×2 trees, trunks 5 blocks apart, 6 blocks between two groups
Needsthe ground may vary by up to 2 blocks over the site
VersionsStandard, Cider press
Level 2works at 150 % pace
Upgrade to 212 planks, 12 ashlars, 2 fittings; 300 work units in 2 stages; once 8 people work and live within 48 blocks of it and it has run 1 day without a problem

The orchard plants oaks on a plantation of its own and picks their apples. A picked tree stays standing and bears again, so an orchard, once grown, is food for good.

  • It lays out its own spots, in groups of four, ring by ring outwards from the building, keeping clear of buildings and roads.
  • It plants a sapling on every free spot, and needs nothing delivered for it. The sapling is an oak ten minutes later, and a day after that apples show in its leaves. Then it is picked for two fruit, and bears again a day later.
  • Waiting for the fruit is not a standstill: the worker stays at the orchard while something is ripening.
  • The settlement grows these trees, not chance. A sapling on an orchard spot does not grow by itself and not by bone meal either; one beside it does, as usual.
  • Fruit is food, the same as bread and fish — see Food. Once the settlement has enough of it, the orchard rests.
  • It costs planks and nothing else, like the fishing hut: food before you have a quarry.

The trees are protected

  • Only the freeholder and the members may take an orchard tree down — its trunk, its leaves, its sapling and the ground of its spot belong to the tree, and that holds even where a spot lies outside your boundary. Anybody else is told “This tree belongs to an orchard of X. Only its freeholder and members may take it down.”
  • A tree you fell yourself frees its spot, and the orchard plants it again.
  • No lumberjack counts or fells an orchard tree, and no forester plants on a spot or within 5 blocks of one.
  • Tear the orchard down and its trees go wild: they stay standing, nothing protects them any more, and a lumberjack may fell them. Apples still in the leaves stay there.
  • An orchard takes over any oak that stands on one of its spots — the trees of an orchard you tore down, if you build a new one in the same place, or a wild oak. It starts to ripen and bears a day later. A tree of any other kind on a spot stays what it is, and the spot stays empty.

Level 2

At level 2 the orchard works at 150 % pace: picking fruit takes 40 seconds instead of 60, planting two thirds of its time. The rules of every upgrade are on Upgrades; the cost and the neighbourhood it needs are in the table at the top of this page.

  • You can tell it by stone and lanterns: its walls are masoned, and lanterns hang in place of torches.
