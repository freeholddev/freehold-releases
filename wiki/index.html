Freehold Wiki

Freehold is a settlement and economy mod for Minecraft — Rule, don't manage. You found a settlement with a charter, set the rules it runs by, and it organises itself: it gives its people work, makes and carries its goods, walks the roads you draw and pushes its boundary out. You decide what is built where and which policy holds, never who carries which log.

This wiki is the reference for all of it: every building, good and tool, every gesture and every message, the rules behind them, and what a server admin can set. New to Freehold? Start with First steps. A word you do not know? Look it up in the Glossary.

Key ideas

  • The settlement runs itself. It decides who works where, what is made next and which carrier takes which load; you set the frame — see Residents.
  • The work is real, the people are a picture of it. The same planks are sawn at the same moment whether you watch or are on the other side of the world.
  • Goods are carried, not teleported. Every load walks from door to door, so where you build decides how fast things flow — see Goods and carriers.
  • Nothing is made without end. Every good has a ceiling that grows with the settlement, so a full store does not stop everything — see Only so much is made.
  • People need a fed bed. Newcomers arrive on their own, but only into a bed with food in the house — see Food.

This version

Freehold is in public beta, at version 0.7.0. It is playable, but it is not finished, and it stays at 0.x until 1.0: a save may not carry over from one version to the next, and only 1.0 makes promises about compatibility.

What is in the game: wood, stone, food, iron, trade and defence. A settlement fells trees, saws planks, makes its own tools, quarries stone, grows wheat, catches fish, picks apples, grinds and bakes, chars charcoal, sinks a mine, smelts iron and forges it, brews beer and pours it in a tavern, and builds houses. Goods are carried from door to door, roads and junctions are drawn by hand, a watchtower pushes the boundary out, and the people have to eat. A village gate connects a neighbouring village, a trade post trades with it for emeralds, and soldiers in watchtowers and barracks fight off the raiders that come for the settlement, and can take the watchtowers of another. A building kept busy in a busy neighbourhood rises to a second level on its own.

Freehold needs Minecraft Java 26.1.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2.122 or newer, and it is installed on the server and on every client. With an older NeoForge the mod loader refuses to start it and names the missing dependency.

The charter is crafted from an emerald framed in eight planks — see First steps.

Every number in this wiki is a starting value from the datapack and may change between versions. Costs, radii, growth times, storage room and the rate of immigration all sit in data/freehold/ and a server can change them without touching the mod — see The datapack.
