# Changelog

One line per version. Freehold is in public beta and stays at `0.x` until `1.0`: save formats
may change between two versions without a migration, and only `1.0` makes promises about
compatibility.

- **0.7.0** (2026-11-12) — **The first public beta.** Found a settlement with a charter and rule
  it from the charter screen. It works wood, stone, food, iron and beer in full chains, carries
  every load from door to door along the roads you draw, and grows on its own. Beyond the
  boundary: a village to connect and trade with, raiders to fight off, other settlements to
  attack. With members and visitors, and themes for another look.
- **0.1.0 to 0.6.0** — development versions, never released.
