# What counts as an asset

**This file carries no license of its own.** Code and assets are under the same terms —
all rights reserved, with the modpack permission — and [LICENSE](LICENSE) states them.

**It draws the one line the modpack permission needs**: derived **assets** may be shipped
with a pack if their origin is named, while the rest of the mod may not be redistributed
outside a pack at all. The line follows the paths inside the mod file,
`freehold-<version>+mc<minecraft version>.jar`.

## Assets

- everything under `assets/freehold/` — textures, models, blockstates, item definitions
  and language files
- the blueprints: the structures under `data/freehold/structure/` and their sources under
  `data/freehold/blueprint_source/`
- the mod logo, `freehold_logo.png`, and the artwork on the store pages

## Not assets

- the compiled code, everything under `io/`
- the rest of `data/freehold/` — goods, buildings, recipes, roads, material classes and
  the other datapack definitions — and the tags under `data/c/` and `data/minecraft/`
- `META-INF/`

Datapack definitions are named separately in the modpack permission: you may ship your
own, which is not the same as redistributing ours.
